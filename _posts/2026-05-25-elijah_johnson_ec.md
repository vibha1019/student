## Before the Event

Before attending the speaker session, I expected to learn about entrepreneurship and what it takes to build a successful company.

## Curiosity Before Attending

I  was especially curious about how Elijah Johnson created SafeBallot and how technology can be used to improve voting accessibility and trust.

## What I Saw

During the session, I listened to Elijah Johnson speak about his experiences as an entrepreneur, athlete, and founder of SafeBallot. He shared stories about building partnerships, developing business ideas, and using technology to help communities

## Most Interesting Thing I Saw

One of the most interesting things I saw was how SafeBallot was used as an educational tool to teach students in Mozambique how to campaign and vote.

## What I Learned

One specific thing I learned was the importance of putting yourself out there and starting before you feel completely ready. Elijah explained that “you don’t have to have the whole staircase to start,” and that success comes from taking initiative even when things feel uncertain. I also learned that rejection is a normal part of entrepreneurship, and that it is more important to focus on the people who believe in your ideas instead of the ones who say no.

## What Inspired Me

I was most inspired by Elijah’s message about just getting started. Sometimes I feel nervous about working on projects or opportunities that I do not already have experience in because I am afraid of failing or not being qualified enough. However, Elijah explained that many successful people begin without having everything figured out, and that growth comes from trying new things and learning through the process.

## Personal Connection

His advice encouraged me to take more risks and be more confident in pursuing ideas and projects that interest me.

## Interaction

**Person or group I learned from:** Presenter

I learned directly from Elijah Johnson during the presentation and Q&A session. He explained how being authentic and building real connections helped him create partnerships and grow his company. He also talked about how people often connect more with personality and passion than just the product itself.

## Depth of Experience

This event changed my thinking by helping me realize that opportunities often come from simply putting yourself out there. It connects to my computer science class because many projects require experimentation, problem-solving, and learning new skills independently. It also relates to my future goals because I want to work in technology and entrepreneurship, where confidence and initiative are important.

## Connection to CS or Future Goals

Overall, this event was meaningful because it gave me a new perspective on entrepreneurship, leadership, and personal growth. Hearing Elijah’s experiences made success feel more realistic and achievable.

## Final Reflection



## Biggest Takeaway

> My biggest takeaway was: You do not need to have everything figured out before you begin — the most important thing is to start and put yourself out there.
